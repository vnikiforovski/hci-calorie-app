What Zalak does

Take a photo, type or say what you ate. Zalak estimates the calories and macros as a range, for example 650–850 kcal, because a photo or a sentence never shows everything. You correct anything that's wrong, then tap Log. Nothing is saved before that, and "Just checking" throws the estimate away.

How sure is Zalak?

  • Sure Clear to see and easy to estimate.
  • Likely Probably right. Check the portion.
  • Unsure Hard to tell, and Zalak says why. In Preferences you choose whether it asks you or makes its best guess.

Estimates, not medical advice

The numbers are estimates to help you notice what you eat. They are not medical or diet advice. Zalak can't see what's hidden in a meal, so it says nothing about allergens: check the label or ask whoever made it.

About

"Zalak" is Macedonian for "a bite". A student project for the course Design of Human-Computer Interaction at FINKI, Skopje, 2026.
