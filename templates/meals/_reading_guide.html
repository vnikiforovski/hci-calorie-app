Why a range?

A photo or a sentence never shows everything: the recipe, the oil, the exact portion. So Zalak gives the lowest and the highest likely amount, for example 650–850 kcal. The real number is most likely in between.

kcal and macros

kcal (kilocalories) measure the energy in food. For scale: an apple is about 80–100 kcal, a slice of bread about 70–90.

Macros are protein, carbs and fat, in grams. Each bar is solid up to the lowest estimate and lighter up to the highest.

How sure is Zalak?

  • Sure Clear to see and easy to estimate.
  • Likely Probably right. Check the portion.
  • Unsure Hard to tell, and Zalak says why. In Preferences you choose whether it asks you or makes its best guess.

Where it comes from

An AI service (Claude) estimates the meal from your words or photo. It's an estimate to help you notice what you eat, not medical advice. Tap the pencil on any food to correct it.
